SpectralCheck-EO — image labelling

RIOTU Lab, Prince Sultan University · 317 images · about 160 minutes

What this is for

We built a dataset of satellite images where the answers were computed automatically from spectral measurements. We now need to know whether a person looking at the picture would agree. Your labels are the check on the automatic method — that is the whole point, so please answer from what you actually see, not from what you think the answer "should" be.

How to do it

  • Look at the satellite image on the left.
  • Answer the question using one of the buttons.
  • Say whether you are confident (high) or unsure (low).
  • Click Next. There are 317 items.
Everyone labels the same images. All of you answer the identical 317 questions — this is not a split workload, and no one is covering a different section. We compare your answers to each other and to the automatic method, and that comparison only works when every item has several independent judgements.

So please answer every item, and do not skip the difficult ones. If an item is hard, mark confidence low; if it cannot be answered from the picture at all, press "cannot answer". Both are useful answers. A skipped item is the only kind that helps nobody.
The most important instruction — please read carefully. There are two different situations and they need two different responses:

1. You are unsure, but you can see something. You are not certain whether that patch is dense or moderate vegetation, but you can see the vegetation. → Pick your best answer and mark confidence "low". Do not press "cannot answer" just because it is a close call.

2. The information is not in the picture at all. Nothing you could look at more carefully would help — for example, being asked about moisture inside plants, which a normal photo simply does not show. → Press "Cannot answer from this image".

The rule: uncertain → low confidence; invisible → cannot answer. Some of these questions were built from infrared measurements that a normal photo does not contain, and finding out which ones is exactly what we are measuring. So "cannot answer" is a real result — but only for case 2.
The colours look unusual, and that is expected. These previews are contrast-stretched per image, so vegetation may appear dark green, magenta or pink rather than natural green. Judge by shape, texture and pattern — fields, forest, water, buildings — not by exact colour.
Orientation. North is up. "Northern part" means the top third of the image, "southeastern" the bottom-right corner, "central" the middle. Each image covers roughly 5 × 5 km.

Please do not

  • Discuss answers with the other labellers while working. Because you are all labelling the same images, agreement between you is one of the things we measure — and it means nothing if you conferred. Compare notes afterwards, not during.
  • Look up the location or use any other tool.
  • Rush. If you get tired, stop and come back — your progress is saved.

Your answers

They are saved in this browser automatically after every click, so closing the tab loses nothing. When you finish, press Download CSV at the top and send the file back. Nothing is uploaded anywhere by this page.
